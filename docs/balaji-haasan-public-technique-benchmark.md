# Balaji Haasan: public prediction technique benchmark

*Research date: 29 September 2026. Scope: videos published on [Balaji Haasan's official channel](https://youtube.com/@balajihaasan-officialchannel), identified by the project owner. This is not an accuracy audit or a claim to know his private consultation method.*

## Executive assessment

Balaji's strongest visible skill is turning a small set of chart and transit inputs into a long, layered, concrete-sounding reading. Three 2026 annual sign forecasts (Aries, Aquarius, Leo), four 2026 election videos (Tamil Nadu statewide and Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Karur districts), and one 2024-25 Jupiter-transit announcement were examined in Tamil automatic captions. The annual videos run roughly 40 minutes and break a generic sign into month, nakshatra/pada, demographic and life-situation branches. Election videos compare named candidates' natal factors and transits, distinguish charts with birth times from date-only charts, and sometimes acknowledge excluded rivals. His output ranges from career, finance and property to spouse, child, father, health and remedies. He makes month-level and sometimes week-level claims, but sign-wide forecasts are not individualized natal readings.

A deterministic app can imitate the *visible method*: compute precise charts and transits, attach a documented rule to each claim, distinguish natal from sign-wide claims, assemble conditional branches, and show uncertainty. It cannot infer his private intuition, actual predictive reliability, or every method he knows from these recordings. The public catalog is dominated by transit forecasts, elections and Q&A, not a confirmed corpus of individual client readings. We should not say that any method makes reliable life predictions: astrology lacks scientific validation, and apparent past hits selected after the fact do not establish a hit rate.

## The reading architecture

### 1. Define audience and limits

He identifies rashi and included birth-star subdivisions, not just an undifferentiated sun-sign audience. Aries lists Ashwini, Bharani and Krittika first pada ([02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kG7YfX5MSj8&t=142s)); Aquarius lists Dhanishta padas 3-4, Shatabhisha and Purva Bhadrapada padas 1-3 ([00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=58s)); Leo lists Magha, Purva Phalguni and Uttara Phalguni first pada ([02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9VjVnfXCwM&t=165s)). This is group segmentation, not evidence he has each viewer's chart.

### 2. Establish broad year-level background, then narrow the clock

Aquarius calls Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu annual or slow-moving planets ([01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=81s)). Leo reasons from Saturn in the eighth from the sign and its third, seventh and tenth aspects to work, income/family and aspirations; he says a single Saturn placement yields many points ([00:42-01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9VjVnfXCwM&t=42s)). He considers Jupiter moving through different houses and apparent reversal across the year ([01:43-02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9VjVnfXCwM&t=103s)). The transcript's movement dates must be independently recalculated before entering an engine.

### 3. Compose topical and time-bounded branches

Aquarius names Sun, Mars, Mercury and Venus as shorter-cycle planets for monthly results ([03:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=223s)); Leo says those planets combining with annual planets produce different outcomes ([02:26-02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9VjVnfXCwM&t=146s)). Aquarius links Jupiter's second/eleventh lordship and transit in the fifth to a May transition ([02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=162s)), advises April as a favorable job-change window ([14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=856s)), and adds a second-week-of-December Rahu boundary ([07:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=452s)). Aries connects a Jupiter change after June with relief in family matters ([05:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kG7YfX5MSj8&t=342s)). These statements illustrate forecast *granularity*, not verified timing skill.

### 4. Subdivide by star, role, life stage and outcome

Aries moves from general and month sections to Ashwini, Bharani and Krittika (~[23:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kG7YfX5MSj8&t=1418s)); Aquarius starts its Dhanishta/Shatabhisha/Purva Bhadrapada section at [21:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=1313s). He branches on employment, business, married/unmarried, previous separation, seeking a child, student, parent arranging a child's marriage, and sometimes gender. Examples: an Aquarius parent may arrange a son or daughter's marriage ([07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=443s)); Aries mentions benefit from paternal relatives ([07:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kG7YfX5MSj8&t=438s)); Leo mentions reconciliation of separated spouses ([05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9VjVnfXCwM&t=357s)). These are claims *about* relatives inferred from the audience's sign, not analyses of each relative's chart.

### 5. Close with remedies and motivational/practical framing

Aquarius lays out positive, adverse and remedial segments and divides remedies by ordinary and VIP circumstances ([04:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=278s)); Aries refers to goals such as work, marriage, children, ancestral property and debt before choosing actions ([02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kG7YfX5MSj8&t=171s)). Leo lists remedies as its final section ([03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9VjVnfXCwM&t=207s)). In a health/fertility segment, Aquarius suggests seeing a gynecologist before trying to conceive ([11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18gXX8iOGFg&t=661s)), alongside unsupported percentages. The app must not launder an unverified astrological medical claim into clinical advice, fertility odds, or treatment.

## Specificity and assessment rubric

| Dimension | Visible level | Test still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Breadth | High: work, wealth, property, spouse, children, family, education, travel, health, spiritual remedies | Measure distinct *non-overlapping* claim families in actual consultation. |
| Temporal precision | Usually year/month, occasionally a named week or ingress; election outcome tied to polling/counting | Record forecast *before* event and score date windows, not hindsight. |
| Person-level resolution | Annual videos: low, even if many subgroups; election charts: named-person but time data often missing | Blind reading of individual known birth charts without feedback or public biographies. |
| Mechanistic transparency | Medium: named planets, houses, lords, nakshatras, aspects, yogas, periods; strength ranking not reproducible | Reproduce each claimed planet/chart and require rule precedence. |
| Family reach | Effects on child, spouse, father/relatives are mentioned | Test family claims against actual relative data with consent; avoid using one person's chart as medical/fateful evidence about another. |
| Calibration | Not established; occasional percentages lack a denominator or audited cohort | Predeclare claim, outcome definition, time window, probability and all misses. |

A conditional statement ('if you are seeking a marriage / already separated / run a business') narrows a group, but it can cover many viewers simultaneously. Count specificity at the level of a forecast that could be proved wrong for a known individual, not the number of details spoken. No score such as "80% as good as Balaji" is defensible from these videos.

## Rule-engine requirements to approximate this *public* method

1. **Inputs and provenance:** birth date/time/place, timezone/DST, geocoding uncertainty, chosen sidereal ayanamsha, ephemeris version and license; label date-only charts explicitly and suppress ascendant, house, exact dasha and sensitive conclusions when unsupported. Save input provenance, rectification status and calculation trace.
2. **Deterministic chart layer:** sidereal longitudes; rashi, Moon nakshatra/pada, lagna when time exists; planets/lords, houses from Moon and ascendant, aspects, dignity, exchanges, yogas and observed retrogression. Model natal/transit relationships and topic-specific house/karaka links. Do not treat a simple count of "strong planets" as a validated Shadbala formula.
3. **Time engine:** compute transits and ingress/retrograde intervals exactly, then branch by year, month and smaller windows. Implement a documented period hierarchy such as Vimshottari maha-/antar-/pratyantar-dasha only after validating birth time and rule source. Do not ascribe that exact period system to Balaji on this evidence alone.
4. **Interpretation grammar:** generate a finding as `(condition, chart evidence, source rule, topic, valence, interval, audience, uncertainty, exceptions)`. Compose general themes, month sections, nakshatra-pada branches, roles/life stage, family claims, remedies. Distinguish a natal claim from a mass-audience Moon-sign claim. Keep contradictory rules visible and define precedence, not an opaque flood of plausible sentences.
5. **Safety:** no deterministic diagnosis, fertility percentage, death prediction, financial guarantee or specific claim about a third party without justified data/consent. Remedies need provenance, feasibility and harm checks; avoid presenting ritual as treatment. Preserve the classics' historical language and flag dated stereotypes.
6. **Evaluation:** freeze the rule set and birth data, collect prospective timestamped claims, score by category, window, falsifiability, correct/incorrect/ambiguous and calibration. Compare against simple baselines (generic sign forecast, common-life-event rates). Publish misses and source citations alongside hits. Keep election/collective predictions as a separate evaluation track.

`books.json` also records that the 1949 Jaimini Sutras annotated edition was removed for rights reasons. The existing text corpus can help formulate a first natal rule inventory, but it does not automatically cover Balaji's full applied judgment, exact ephemeris calculations, recent political context, or a measured success rate. Some cited texts have source/copy restrictions even if a scan is labeled public domain; public-product reuse and Swiss Ephemeris licensing need separate rights review.

## Follow-up needed for a genuine full skill assessment

Find, with permission, at least 5-10 complete individual readings of varied clients and accessible chart inputs, preferably recorded before the claimed events. Annotate each atomic past/future claim, date window, source technique, client feedback elicited, uncertainty, and observed result. Balance examples across career, marriage, children, family and health, including wrong calls. Independently reproduce the charts and compare a blind implementation of the curated book rules against a simple base-rate control. Until then, call this the **public forecast and chart-comparison benchmark**, not a test of private consultations or proven predictive ability.
