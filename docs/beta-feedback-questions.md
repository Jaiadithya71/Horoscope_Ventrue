# Beta feedback questions (8-9 testers)

Send these after the tester has used the app. Plain words, answerable on a phone in about 3 minutes. Do not tell testers what the chart "should" say.

Before they start, tell them: this is a research preview. It reads themes from one classical text. It does not predict events. Birth time matters: if they are unsure, use the "not sure of the exact time" option.

## Ask in this order

1. How sure were you of your birth time? (exact / within an hour / not sure at all). Which option did you use?
2. Did the lagna and Moon star match what you already know from your own chart? (yes / no / do not know)
3. Read "The person behind the chart". Did it feel like you? Mark each line: fits / partly / does not fit.
4. What felt wrong or off? Quote the line if you can.
5. Looking at your current stretch of life (the dates shown): does the overview match what the last few years felt like? (yes / partly / no)
6. Which of the six areas (work, money, health, relationships, family, mind) felt closest to true? Which was furthest?
7. What did you want more of? (more dates, more detail, more advice, something else)
8. Anything that confused you, or that you could not find? Did the 60-second story make sense, or did you prefer the full page?
9. Did anything scare or upset you? Which line?
10. Would you send this to a friend? Why or why not?

## How to log answers

One row per tester: tester id (T1 to T9), birth-time sureness, lagna match, per-line fit marks, wrong lines quoted, wanted more of, confusion, upset line. Do not record birth details with the answers.

## What the answers can and cannot show

Fit marks show whether the wording resonates. They do not show the reading is accurate. Wording that fits almost everyone (a Barnum effect) will also score high, so ask for the "does not fit" lines as much as the "fits" ones.
