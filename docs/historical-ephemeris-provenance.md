# Historical ephemeris provenance

The Sripati-Sastri scan PDF72/printed58 explicitly says Cheshtakendra needs Manda/Sighra astronomy and names V.B.Ketkar's Jyotirganitam tables. PDF73/printed59 gives five non-luminary angles attributed to those tables. This citation does not identify the exact edition, row set, input date/time or coordinate branch used sufficiently for automatic reconstruction.

The [downloaded Jyotir Ganita scan](https://archive.org/details/jyotir-ganita-venkatesh-ramakrishna-kethkar-dattatreya-ketkar-surakant-jha) PDF22-23 has a publisher note calling it a second edition, saying matter was revised, tables enlarged and the father's Sanskrit supplement incorporated. It is signed D.V.Ketkar,16August1937. PDF24's author preface names Leverrier, Hansen, Chatre and Newcomb as resources and describes correcting older astronomy. These pages were visually inspected. A [separately fetched archive text](https://archive.org/stream/Jyotirganitam/Jyotirganitam_djvu.txt) corroborates the revised-second-edition note.

This establishes source lineage and an edition-mismatch risk, not a complete historical ephemeris. The downloaded reissue is not silently assumed identical to the table set used for Sripati's worked angles. Nor is generic modern mean longitude or instantaneous speed a substitute for this cited system.

Next reconstruction must identify the relevant table/epoch, calendar and local time reference, mean/true/Sighrochcha conventions, planetary-centre corrections, coordinate unwrapping, and independently reproduce at least one full angle chain before enabling chart-derived III motion. Published corrected/enlarged tables require edition-specific validation. IV luminary rays remain separate from III motion.
