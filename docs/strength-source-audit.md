The Sun declination example is892.737 arcminutes north. Its undoubled formula gives0.809978125 Rupa, consistent with the printed0.8099/rounded0.810; the explicit doubling instruction yields1.61995625. These are source disagreement, not a rounding difference. The code keeps the disagreement.

[Phaladeepika IV.1-3, PDF70-72](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.92117) uses a different sixfold enumeration and night/day classifications. The two books are not one interchangeable formula. Its original translator points to Sripati II-III for details (PDF80), but that does not erase disagreement or permit mixing all components into an undocumented total.

Still absent: full positional aggregation with compound relationships and house geometry, numerical motion using the book's mean/true-motion definitions, complete temporal lord/third-of-day pieces, numerical aspect adjustment, planetary war, combustion thresholds, a source-chosen complete profile and global outcome priority. Personal-outcome rules remain disabled. Tests check text mechanics and arithmetic, not scientific predictive validity or Balaji's private practice.

## Degree bhava geometry

Sripati I.6-8, PDF25-26 (printed11-12), image-inspected, trisects the forward quadrants between supplied ascendant/fourth/seventh/tenth anchors; adjacent centres' midpoint is their Sandhi. The helper implements these centre/boundary calculations, wrap-aware, with exact-boundary membership held unresolved. It does not yet supply or certify astronomical anchors. A printed arithmetic discrepancy is tested: the worked paragraph's third centre2-9-53-43 disagrees with its table's2-9-58-43 (PDF27). Exact trisection gives2-9-58-42.6667, matching the table to rounding. Do not imitate the paragraph typo.

Natal integration now obtains ascendant/MC from the existing Lahiri Swiss Ephemeris angle tuple. The [library source](https://github.com/aloistr/swisseph/blob/c353e6f8/swehouse.c), inspected live, names angle0 ascendant and1 MC and exposes the sidereal transformation. The code adds, not replaces, Sripati memberships and feeds those centre longitudes to Digbala. Invalid/reversed/degenerate quadrants do not produce a substitute score. Traditional rising-time tables from the book are not represented as identical to the modern astronomical model.

Rasi/Navamsa parity is now an implemented positional subcomponent from III.4, image-inspected PDF49-50 (printed35-36): Moon/Venus get1/4 Rupa for each even sign or even Navamsa, other classical planets for each odd sign/Navamsa. Source Sun Aries/Virgo example0.25, Moon Capricorn/Taurus0.5 and Mars Pisces/Pisces0 reproduced. This does not close full positional strength or its seven-varga/compound-relationship aggregation.

III.5 (PDF51-52, printed37-38), image-inspected: Kendra/Kantaka1, Panapara1/2 and Apoklima1/4 Rupa. Commentary explicitly disagrees on whether these are Rasi or degree-Bhava houses, so both input-derived candidates are retained and disagreement flagged; exact Sandhi or unavailable geometry supplies no degree candidate. Decan component gives1/4 Rupa in first/second/third decan for masculine/neuter/feminine historical planet categories. The categories are independently image-checked in Phaladeepika II.27 PDF56-57: Sun/Mars/Jupiter masculine, Mercury/Saturn neuter, Moon/Venus feminine. The Sripati commentary points to Jatakaparijata II.27, not that Phaladeepika passage; cross-source provenance stays explicit. These are historical symbolic planet labels, not statements about people. Quoted own-Shadvarga30-Virupa refinement remains unevaluated rather than guessing its scope; it would change Jupiter's printed table0.25 to0.5. No refinement or whole score is silently applied.

PDF53 (printed39), image-inspected, shows the five positional pieces collected together: Uchcha, seven-varga, parity, house-category and decan. Four partial numerical pieces now exist; seven-varga/compound-relation calculation remains missing and no complete positional sum is emitted. Printed subcomponent/table discrepancies remain evidence to audit, not a reason to treat incomplete pieces as a total. README and machine-readable missing-component status were reconciled with current code so implemented degree geometry/Digbala is no longer called absent.

Added separate seven-varga owner geometry without changing the Phaladeepika six-varga output. Sripati III.3 PDF40 lists Saptamsa among its seven divisions but does not fully define geometry there. Sarvartha Chintamani I.19, original1899 Part1 PDF44-45 (printed26-27), image-inspected from the live Drive source, supplies seven equal divisions, own-sign start for odd signs and seventh-sign start for even. The printed Aries/Taurus owner sequences and Sripati's Sun17deg43min30sec/Leo Saptamsa are reproduced. This is explicit cross-source geometry, not numeric seven-varga strength. Sripati PDF45-48 also explicitly contrasts direct planet-to-owner relationship scoring with an alternate score based on the owner's own placement; Sun2.5 versus2.75 in its examples. No choice is hidden and no numeric aggregate is enabled.
