The Sun declination example is892.737 arcminutes north. Its undoubled formula gives0.809978125 Rupa, consistent with the printed0.8099/rounded0.810; the explicit doubling instruction yields1.61995625. These are source disagreement, not a rounding difference. The code keeps the disagreement.

[Phaladeepika IV.1-3, PDF70-72](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.92117) uses a different sixfold enumeration and night/day classifications. The two books are not one interchangeable formula. Its original translator points to Sripati II-III for details (PDF80), but that does not erase disagreement or permit mixing all components into an undocumented total.

Still absent: full positional aggregation with compound relationships and house geometry, numerical motion using the book's mean/true-motion definitions, automatic temporal lord/third-of-day integration, signed net aspect adjustment, planetary war, combustion thresholds, a source-chosen complete profile and global outcome priority. Personal-outcome rules remain disabled. Tests check text mechanics and arithmetic, not scientific predictive validity or Balaji's private practice.

## Degree bhava geometry

Sripati I.6-8, PDF25-26 (printed11-12), image-inspected, trisects the forward quadrants between supplied ascendant/fourth/seventh/tenth anchors; adjacent centres' midpoint is their Sandhi. The helper implements these centre/boundary calculations, wrap-aware, with exact-boundary membership held unresolved. It does not yet supply or certify astronomical anchors. A printed arithmetic discrepancy is tested: the worked paragraph's third centre2-9-53-43 disagrees with its table's2-9-58-43 (PDF27). Exact trisection gives2-9-58-42.6667, matching the table to rounding. Do not imitate the paragraph typo.

Natal integration now obtains ascendant/MC from the existing Lahiri Swiss Ephemeris angle tuple. The [library source](https://github.com/aloistr/swisseph/blob/c353e6f8/swehouse.c), inspected live, names angle0 ascendant and1 MC and exposes the sidereal transformation. The code adds, not replaces, Sripati memberships and feeds those centre longitudes to Digbala. Invalid/reversed/degenerate quadrants do not produce a substitute score. Traditional rising-time tables from the book are not represented as identical to the modern astronomical model.

Rasi/Navamsa parity is now an implemented positional subcomponent from III.4, image-inspected PDF49-50 (printed35-36): Moon/Venus get1/4 Rupa for each even sign or even Navamsa, other classical planets for each odd sign/Navamsa. Source Sun Aries/Virgo example0.25, Moon Capricorn/Taurus0.5 and Mars Pisces/Pisces0 reproduced. This does not close full positional strength or its seven-varga/compound-relationship aggregation.

III.5 (PDF51-52, printed37-38), image-inspected: Kendra/Kantaka1, Panapara1/2 and Apoklima1/4 Rupa. Commentary explicitly disagrees on whether these are Rasi or degree-Bhava houses, so both input-derived candidates are retained and disagreement flagged; exact Sandhi or unavailable geometry supplies no degree candidate. Decan component gives1/4 Rupa in first/second/third decan for masculine/neuter/feminine historical planet categories. The categories are independently image-checked in Phaladeepika II.27 PDF56-57: Sun/Mars/Jupiter masculine, Mercury/Saturn neuter, Moon/Venus feminine. The Sripati commentary points to Jatakaparijata II.27, not that Phaladeepika passage; cross-source provenance stays explicit. These are historical symbolic planet labels, not statements about people. Quoted own-Shadvarga30-Virupa refinement remains unevaluated rather than guessing its scope; it would change Jupiter's printed table0.25 to0.5. No refinement or whole score is silently applied.

PDF53 (printed39), image-inspected, shows the five positional pieces collected together: Uchcha, seven-varga, parity, house-category and decan. Four partial numerical pieces now exist; automatic source-selected seven-varga/compound-relation integration remains missing and no complete positional sum is emitted. Printed subcomponent/table discrepancies remain evidence to audit, not a reason to treat incomplete pieces as a total. README and machine-readable missing-component status were reconciled with current code so implemented degree geometry/Digbala is no longer called absent.

Added separate seven-varga owner geometry without changing the Phaladeepika six-varga output. Sripati III.3 PDF40 lists Saptamsa among its seven divisions but does not fully define geometry there. Sarvartha Chintamani I.19, original1899 Part1 PDF44-45 (printed26-27), image-inspected from the live Drive source, supplies seven equal divisions, own-sign start for odd signs and seventh-sign start for even. The printed Aries/Taurus owner sequences and Sripati's Sun17deg43min30sec/Leo Saptamsa are reproduced. This is explicit cross-source geometry, not numeric seven-varga strength. Sripati PDF45-48 also explicitly contrasts direct planet-to-owner relationship scoring with an alternate score based on the owner's own placement; Sun2.5 versus2.75 in its examples. No choice is hidden. An explicit-input direct-owner numerical helper was added later; automatic profile selection remains disabled.

Sripati II.2-6 PDF31-34 (printed17-20), image-inspected, now supports a separately named numerical degree-aspect table profile. The directed longitude excess is interpolated linearly between quarter-unit knots:30:0,60:1,90:3,120:2,150:0,180:4,210:3,240:2,270:1,300:0; outside30..300 zero. Saturn promotes excess2/9signs to4quarters; Mars3/7; Jupiter4/8. Jupiter's PDF34 prose erroneously says3/4, but the explicit worked table, II.5's fifth/ninth places and4sign16degree worked example agree on4/8; this conflict is exposed in the Jupiter output. Tests reproduce Sun-to-Moon0.277 and Jupiter-to-Sun0.456 (base0.228 plus tapered0.228) from the original worked arithmetic. The quoted Parashara PDF35-36 formula stays outside this profile. Unsigned pair amounts do not constitute signed Drigbala or full strength; no favorable/malefic sign or net sum is invented.

Kapoor Phaladeepika II.23 commentary PDF22-23, image-inspected, supplies all six natural-plus-temporary relation combinations and explicitly labels remaining positions temporal enemies. Source: https://jyotishvidya.com/HTMLobj-9415/Mantreswara_s__Phaladeeplka_.pdf . The older1937 translation states friends but does not spell out this complete table; no claim is made that it does. Sripati III.3 PDF41/43 explicitly discusses the latter two Bhava methods and rejects the recast in its examples. Added an explicit-input direct-owner seven-varga component, from III.2-3 PDF38/40 and worked Sun PDF44-46:3/4 Rasi Moolatrikona,1/2 own,3/8 very friend,1/4 friend,1/8 neutral,1/16 enemy,1/32 very enemy. Caller must supply grounded compound relations and Rasi Moolatrikona condition; missing inputs abstain. The owner-placement alternative and its2.75 example remain exposed and unimplemented. No automatic natal seven-varga or positional total is emitted.

## Temporal thirds and lords

Sripati III.13-14 PDF60-64 (printed46-50), directly image-inspected, supports supplied-input helpers in `temporal_lords.py`. Day thirds Mercury/Sun/Saturn, night Moon/Venus/Mars get1 Rupa for the active lord, Jupiter always1. Inputs are fractions of actual supplied solar day/night, never civil-clock thirds; exact internal boundaries abstain. Year/month/day/hour lord contributions are1/4,1/2,3/4,1. The historical year/month calculation divides supplied terrestrial elapsed days by360/30, not Gregorian calendar fields. Source714,404,106,135 days reproduces quotients1,984,455,850 and23,813,470,204, remainders5/6/6, Jupiter/Venus/Venus. Sunday counts1, Saturday0. No modern historical epoch solver supplied.

The24-equal-part Kala-hora cycle is Jupiter/Mars/Sun/Venus/Mercury/Moon/Saturn, weekday lord first. Caller must ground day origin/fraction; no modern civil-clock or seasonal-hour substitution. The separate half-Rasi alternative doubles the wrapped Ascendant-minus-Sun arc and reduces completed signs mod7. Printed sample gives23sign23deg36min32sec, remainder2: zero-based offset gives Moon, matching the printed planet, but this is third from Venus, not the printed fourth. One-based remainder would give Mercury. Both candidates and the ordinal conflict remain visible; no automatic selection or full temporal total.

Current limits replace the older incremental descriptions: unsigned aspects, explicit-input direct-owner seven-varga and temporal-third/lord helpers now exist. Full positional/temporal profile selection, mean/true-motion integration, signed aspect aggregation, war/combustion and global outcome priority remain open.
