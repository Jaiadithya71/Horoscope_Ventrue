# Kumaraswamiyam: source and extraction record

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition

The Tamil Digital Library catalogue identifies TVA_BOK_0041239 as the astrology work by Veeravanallur Kumaraswami Desikar, original and commentary, Chennai Vidyaratnakara Press, 1923. It credits Vallinayagam Pillai and M. Krishnappillai in the editing/publication chain. Catalogue extent: 6 + 273 printed pages. Downloaded PDF extent: 283 scan pages. The original cover image visibly prints 1923; this is an old-print scan, not the 2007 Sarasvati Mahal commentary linked on Scribd.

The classical text and this 1923 printed source were selected as the old-edition route. No later translation or commentary has been imported. The PDF's 2023 creation metadata is the digitisation date, not the edition date. This record is not a legal opinion about every jurisdiction.

## Coverage and quality

- 283/283 scan pages produced OCR output, each kept under its own page heading.
- 0/283 pages have a fully proofread, line-by-line transcription.
- Contents headings and main section start pages were checked directly against PDF pages 2-3; see the separate checked outline.
- The machine text has substantial mistakes, including extra marks from background noise, broken Tamil words, and confused page/verse numerals. Complete scan coverage does not mean complete accurate text.
- Scan cover annotations, library marks, footnotes and tables may be mixed into the OCR.
- No English translation, normalised edition, missing-line reconstruction, astrology calculation or new prediction rule was made.

## Avoiding look-alikes

The user-linked Scribd record TVA_BOK_0008544 is a different edition: Sarasvati Mahal publication 497, first edition 2007, edited by Sathyabama Kameswaran, xxiv + 332 printed pages, 358 Scribd pages. It was not transcribed here.

The archive.org item TVA_BOK_0003064, Sri-mat Kumaraswamiyam (1925), associated with Satchidanandam Pillai/Pamban Swamigal, is a similarly titled devotional work and was excluded. Same title is not proof of the same astrology text.

## What this adds

Unlike the earlier practitioner-video/display notes and the app's individually cited Sanskrit arithmetic sources, this is a primary Tamil astrology-source candidate with a visible structure spanning foundation, natal, electional and enquiry sections. The inspected index includes Ashtakavarga, Kalachakra, house-specific natal chapters, dasha, marriage matching and travel. These are research leads, not extracted executable rules. OCR must never be treated as original-page verification.
