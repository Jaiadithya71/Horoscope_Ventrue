## Format pattern, vs the astro__tamil model (see instagram-tamil-astro-reels-2026-10-18.md)
- Same underlying engine as the IG references: sign identity. But the mechanics differ:
  - astro__tamil asks an identity question and closes with "comment your sign" (its 1.7M-view reel: 791 comments / 41.3K likes, a ~1.9% comment-to-like loop; 1,980 reposts).
  - This reel has no question and no CTA: 87 comments on 9.8K likes (~0.9%) and 314 shares (~3.2% of likes). Like rate on views is comparable (1.8% here vs 2.4% on the astro__tamil 1.7M reel).
- The identity hook moved into the caption's first person, and the cute mascot visual does the emotional work the question used to do. Production cost is minimal: one AI animation, 12 s, no editing, no voiceover, no list content.
- No comment CTA means a weaker comment loop; its reach leans on the shareable visual and Facebook reel distribution instead.
- Fit for our astro__tamil-style batch: a hybrid is the takeaway - mascot-style AI visual + one identity line + an explicit "comment your rasi" CTA, rather than copying either template whole. As before: learn the pattern, never the content (strikes/account-loss risk on re-uploads stands).
