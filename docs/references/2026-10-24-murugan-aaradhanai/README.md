## Format analysis (benchmark relevance for astro_guidance_108)

- 45–110s shorts, one continuous Tamil voiceover telling a single rule/remedy; info is
  spoken, text overlays are decorative.
- Hook formula: fear/fortune claim + hyper-specific number ("16-generation curse",
  "₹10 item", "24 generations", "these 5 rasis"). Numbers make it concrete.
- Identity targeting by rasi — viewers wait to hear their own sign (the same mechanic
  as the astro__tamil weekday/nakshatra benchmarks already in this directory).
- Comment CTA is devotional ("comment Om Namasivaya / Om Muruga") — cheap, on-topic,
  and it seeds the comments with affirmation.
- Ruthless festival timing: clusters of shorts on the same tithi for days before it
  (Aippasi Pournami got 7+ shorts in 3 days), each a slightly different angle
  (what to buy, what not to buy, what not to donate, the one "secret" to do).
- Claim-risk note for our own channel: this channel freely makes health/curse/money
  guarantees — exactly the claim categories we decided to avoid on astro_guidance_108.
  Model the mechanics (hook, identity, timing, CTA), not the claims.
